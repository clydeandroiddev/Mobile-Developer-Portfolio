### VASK (Voters ASK) for Android and iOS
(Store listing was removed after the Philippine Elections due to data privacy.)

VASK helped on-site PPCRV volunteers assist voters during the May 2022 Philippine Elections. Volunteers used it to find a voter's clustered precinct within their assigned municipality and voting center. Besides exact search, users could limit the search to specific voting centers or run a wildcard search on a voter's initials. The app also had a help module and a connectivity check for the first download of the VASK database.
